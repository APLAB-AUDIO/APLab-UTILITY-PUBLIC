# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-24)
- First public release of Hotkey (formerly "Encoder"): keyboard, dial, mouse wheel, game controller and MIDI control of any module, with slots, groups, pages, remaps, push links, custom keys, sensitivity, undo and a dial setup wizard.
- Extra dials: up to 4 additional learned knobs for keyboards and macropads with more than one dial.
- Hold several slot numbers and turn the dial to move all of those knobs together.
- AP Lab light and dark panels.
